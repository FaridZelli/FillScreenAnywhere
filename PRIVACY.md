# Fill Screen Anywhere Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026  
Developer: Farid Zellipour  
Source code: https://github.com/FaridZelli/FillScreenAnywhere

Fill Screen Anywhere does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any user data.

## Data Collection
Fill Screen Anywhere does not collect personal information, browsing history, page content, video data, IP addresses, device identifiers, usage statistics, or analytics of any kind. No third-party services, analytics, or external data processing are used.

## Permissions
Fill Screen Anywhere only requests the `storage` permission and access to `<all_urls>`. Content scripts operate locally to enable video zoom functionality. User settings (website allow/deny lists, feature toggles) are stored offline in the browser’s local storage. This data never leaves the user's device and can be cleared by removing the extension or restoring defaults.

## Commitment to Privacy & Policy Changes
Fill Screen Anywhere is developed and maintained solely by its original developer, Farid Zellipour. I have no intention of introducing data collection, tracking, or third-party analytics to this extension in the future. 

If this privacy policy is ever updated, the revised version will be posted here with an updated "Last updated" date.
